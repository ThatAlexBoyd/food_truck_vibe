# Sandwich Hero 🥪

## 🤖 A "Vibe Coded" Experiment

Welcome to Sandwich Hero. This project is an experiment in "vibe coding" — a collaborative process between a human prompter and Google's Gemini AI. All game logic, UI code, and descriptive copy you see here have been generated by the AI based on a series of high-level design prompts.

Human intervention has been kept to a minimum, primarily to correct syntax errors or fatal logic flaws ("hallucinations") that prevented the code from compiling or running. As a result, the gameplay is not yet optimized or balanced. It serves as a fascinating look into AI-assisted game prototyping, where the core "vibe" and mechanics are built iteratively through conversation.

---

## 🕹️ Gameplay

Sandwich Hero is a strategic management game where your goal is to travel the region, fill your cookbook with every unique recipe, and become the ultimate Sandwich Hero by mastering them all.

### The Gameplay Loop

1.  **Plan Your Day:** Check your location and use the **Cookbook** to select two sandwiches for your daily menu. The key to success is catering to local tastes. Pay close attention to a location's **Preferred Class** (e.g., `Sub`, `Wrap`) and their specific **Local Favorites**.
2.  **Choose an Action:** From the main panel, decide what you want to do:
    *   **🚚 Setup Shop & Sell:** This is your primary way to earn money and reputation. The game simulates a day of sales based on your menu choices, the location's preferences, and your recipe levels.
    *   **🛒 Visit Store:** Restock your seven core ingredients.
    *   **👨‍🍳 Talk to the Local Expert:** Every location has a resident expert with a unique recipe to teach you... if you can meet their conditions.
    *   **🏘️ Talk to Locals:** Gather intel on which sandwich classes and specific recipes are popular in the area to maximize your profits and reputation.
    *   **📋 Check Bulletin Board:** Discover and complete local quests for extra cash and unique rewards.
    *   **📍 Change Location:** Travel to a new city to find new customers, recipes, and challenges.
3.  **Learn & Master:** Selling sandwiches that match local preferences earns you more XP and Reputation. As a recipe levels up, its selling price increases. Mastered recipes are a reliable way to win over any city.
4.  **Grow Your Empire:** Use your earnings to buy new ingredients, unlock new recipes, and travel to new locations with bigger customer bases and greater challenges.

### Winning the Game

The game is won when you have:
1.  Unlocked every recipe in the game.
2.  Mastered every single recipe to Level 3.

## 🚀 How to Play

No installation or setup is required.

1.  Clone or download this repository.
2.  Open the `sandwich_truck.html` file in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).
3.  That's it! Start your journey to becoming a Sandwich Hero.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

*   **HTML5**
*   **CSS3** (with **Tailwind CSS** via CDN for styling)
*   **Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)** for all game logic.
*   **Font Awesome** & **Google Fonts** (via CDN) for icons and typography.
